# Credibility Report: Coronavirus disease (COVID-19) - Canada.ca

**Source URL:** https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/diseases/coronavirus-disease-covid-19.html

## Executive Summary
This source is **highly credible** overall. It is hosted on **Canada.ca**, the official Government of Canada domain, and appears to be institutionally published by the **Public Health Agency of Canada (PHAC)**. The page is a public-information hub about COVID-19 rather than an opinion piece. Its tone is factual and service-oriented, and it links to official government resources and data.

Important limitations: I did **not** identify a named individual author on the retrieved page, and I could **not** confirm an original publication date from the page text I accessed. However, search results for the exact URL indicate the page was **modified on 2025-08-27**, which supports that it is being maintained. Broad public-health claims on the page are consistent with guidance from the **World Health Organization (WHO)**.
